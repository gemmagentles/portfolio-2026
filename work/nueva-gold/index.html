Case study · Nueva Gold

A custom WordPress website

Nueva Gold is a marketing agency that acts as an in-house team for global brands. At the last agency I worked for, I ran the project and built its front end and custom WordPress theme with Advanced Custom Fields.

The brief

What I delivered

  • A full CMS website: the team can now edit on their own to show prospects their latest work
  • A contact form: a direct route for enquiries that brings in new business
  • Launch-ready: delivered to the original timeline, 97+ Lighthouse performance

Problem solving

The challenges that came

  • Empty buttons: many led nowhere, so I mapped every link to agree cuts with client and designer
  • A halved deadline: I onboarded a new developer; earlier pull requests would have saved a rebuild
  • Doubled-up images: I had the CMS match the desktop site, so the alternating 50/50 rows needed CSS to reorder for phones
