Fraser Valley

Case study · Fraser Valley

CMS website with design

Fraser Valley Gaels is the Vancouver Gaelic football club I play for. I designed and built its WordPress site pro bono, and three years on, the volunteers who run the club still update it themselves.

The brief

What I delivered

  • Recruit players: a site that makes us look the most professional club
  • Credit and celebrate: committee profiles, past players, trophies and sponsor logos
  • Stay current: a custom CMS with multiple user accounts, so volunteers can update it each season

Problem solving

The challenges that came

  • A tall logo left awkward space: I centred it in the nav between hard-coded links to reduce empty space.
  • Some components are on multiple pages, but with design variations: instead of duplicating code, I reused components and just added optional fields
  • Components with optional parts left gaps when empty: thinking site-wide, I wrapped optional fields in PHP ifs and made CSS spacing run one way
