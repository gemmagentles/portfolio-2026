01

Blue and pink carry the brand, mostly as one gradient. Pages alternate black and white sections; the greys are for borders and quiet text on black.

02

Typography

Oswald for all headings (h1, h2 and h3) and the logo; Inter for paragraphs, links and lists; Space Mono for sub headings. Sizes are fluid: each grows from its phone size at 360px wide to its full size at 1280px.

Families

  • Front end developerOswald--font-heading--primaryPage titles (h1), like the hero heading, and the logo
  • Who I've worked withOswald--font-heading--secondarySection headings (h2) and h3
  • Clean, accessible front-end code.Inter--font-body, --font-linkParagraphs, all links and buttons, and lists
  • Chapter II · ClientsSpace Mono--font-monoSub headings (eyebrows, labels), captions and counters

Scale

  • --text-display45 → 112pxDisplay
  • --text-3xl36 → 72pxHeading 1
  • --text-2xl28 → 45pxHeading 2
  • --text-xl23 → 32pxTaglines and card titles
  • --text-large19 → 24pxIntro text and h3
  • --text-body16 → 18pxBody text, set at a line height of 1.6 for comfortable reading.
  • --text-small13 → 15pxCaptions, labels and legal text.

03

Spacing

All padding, margins and gaps come from this scale. Sections add 20px extra at the bottom; the page sides use --gutter, 24px on phones up to 64px.

  • --space-tiny4px
  • --space-xsmall8px
  • --space-small16px
  • --space-medium24px
  • --space-large40px
  • --space-section48 → 80px
  • --space-section-large72 → 144px

Shape, motion & layout

Corners

  • 4pxSmall tiles
  • 8pxCards, screens
  • 12px--radius
  • PillButtons, tags

Motion

--ease-out
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Snappy: page reveals, header links, carousel moves.
Soft ease-out
cubic-bezier(0.33, 1, 0.68, 1). Long glides: the logo intro.
ease-in-out
Colour fades and hover fills, 0.3 to 0.45s.
Reduced motion
Every animation is wrapped so it only runs for visitors who haven't asked for less motion; they see the finished state.
