Chapter IX · Why it matters

My key engineering values.

  1. Build to scale

    Using version control, documentation, naming conventions, variables and reusable components. The site can grow with your business and the development team, futureproofing your build.

  2. Test for all

    The target user audience is considered in depth when making final decisions. But accessibility standards, cross-browser compatibility and AI agents experience must be considered.

  3. Document Workflows

    Acceptance criteria, comments, README, changelog, pull requests, live design systems and live documentation. A source of all truth to reduce friction, either between teams or your future development.
