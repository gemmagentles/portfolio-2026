Chapter IX · Why it matters

My key engineering values.

  1. Build to scale

    I build every project as if it will grow, with version control, clear naming, variables and reusable components. That way a site can grow with your business and your team, instead of needing a rebuild.

  2. Test for all

    I’m a big advocate for accessibility. I believe every role in creating a UI needs to understand accessibility (a11y), so we can hold each other accountable. With AI, it’s easier than ever to run regular automated tests on design and code.

  3. Document workflows

    I believe documentation is part of the build. Acceptance criteria, READMEs, changelogs and live design systems give everyone a single source of truth, so teams work smoothly and the next developer can pick it up.
